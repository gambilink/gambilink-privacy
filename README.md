# Privacy Policy for GambiLink

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

GambiLink respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and handled when you use the GambiLink mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use GambiLink, we may collect information that you provide directly, including:

- Name and profile information
- Email address
- Profile photo
- Account information
- Listings and advertisements you create
- Property listings
- Job applications and job-related information
- Business directory information
- Photos and images you choose to upload
- Messages sent between users
- Favorites and other interactions with listings

We may also process technical information necessary for authentication, security, and operation of the application.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through GambiLink to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users
- Display user profiles where appropriate
- Allow users to create and manage marketplace listings
- Provide property listings
- Provide job listings and job applications
- Provide the business directory
- Enable communication between users
- Save favorites
- Provide notifications
- Improve the functionality and security of GambiLink
- Prevent misuse, fraud, and unauthorized access

## 3. Photos and Media

GambiLink may allow you to select and upload photos from your device, including profile photos, marketplace images, property images, and business images.

GambiLink only accesses photos that you choose to provide through the application's available photo-selection features.

## 4. Account and Authentication

GambiLink uses account authentication to provide access to personalized features.

Account and application data may be processed and stored using third-party infrastructure and backend services required to operate GambiLink.

## 5. Data Sharing

GambiLink does not sell your personal information.

Certain information that you intentionally publish, such as marketplace listings, property listings, business information, and related images, may be visible to other GambiLink users.

Information may also be processed by service providers used to operate the application, subject to their applicable privacy and security practices.

We may disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the application, or our legal rights.

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled through GambiLink. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide GambiLink's services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security of the service.

Users can request or perform account deletion through the account/settings features available in GambiLink.

When an account is deleted, associated account data is deleted or handled according to the application's data-retention requirements, except where information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, or regulatory reasons.

## 8. Children's Privacy

GambiLink is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

## 9. Third-Party Services

GambiLink may rely on third-party services to provide infrastructure, authentication, database storage, file storage, and other functionality.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

## 10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to access, correct, or request deletion of certain information.

GambiLink also provides account-management features that allow users to update certain profile information and delete their account.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when GambiLink's features, practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
