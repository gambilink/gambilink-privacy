# GambiLink – Account and Data Deletion

GambiLink users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

## Delete Your Account in the GambiLink App

To delete your account:

1. Open the GambiLink app.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Go to **My Account**.
4. Open **Settings**.
5. Select **Delete Account**.
6. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Your account will be deleted along with associated account data handled by GambiLink.

## If You Cannot Access the App

If you cannot access your GambiLink account or the app, you can request account deletion by email.

Send your request to:

**gambilink@gmail.com**

Use the subject: **Account Deletion Request**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your GambiLink account so that we can verify the request.

## Data That Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, data associated with your account is deleted as applicable, including:

- Profile and account information
- Listings associated with your account
- Registered businesses associated with your account
- Job applications associated with your account
- Saved items and favorites
- Other account-associated application data

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory obligations. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as required for those purposes.
